## Thesis goal

To develop an application for people without medical education that helps them cope with a difficult emergency situation they witness.

## Feedback and decisions

- **Platforms:** iOS and Android.
- **Cloud vs offline:** the app may use the cloud, but help is also needed where there is no network – a topic to consider and discuss in the thesis.
- **Scientific method is the most important part of the thesis:** research, measurements, then conclusions, possibly comparisons.
- **Models:** compare different models, cloud-based and local.
- **Infrastructure risk:** functionality may be lost when an external infrastructure becomes unavailable (EU–US tensions); a local Polish model (e.g. Bielik) reduces this risk.
- **Literature review:** a complete overview is expected.
- **Order of work:** plan the architecture first, implement afterwards.
- **Meetings every two weeks:** current results plus a discussion of the next step.
